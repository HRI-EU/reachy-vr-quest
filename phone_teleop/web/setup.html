Reachy teleop: one-time setup

Safari only allows motion sensors on HTTPS pages. This laptop runs its own small certificate authority. Install it on the iPhone once:

  1. Download certificate
    Tap “Allow”.
  2. Open Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install.
  3. Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings: switch on Reachy Phone Teleop local CA.
  4. Open {{HTTPS_URL}} in Safari.

The certificate is generated on this laptop and never leaves your network. Remove it any time under Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
