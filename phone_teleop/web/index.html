Reachy Mini teleop

Hold the phone in landscape in front of your eyes. Tap Center while looking straight ahead, then press and hold anywhere on the screen to make the robot follow. Let go to stop.

First time: turn off Portrait Orientation Lock (Control Center), then Motors on → Wake up in the ⋯ menu.

Tip: Share → Add to Home Screen gives a full-screen app.
